# Copyright, licensing, and commercial distribution policy

New application code in this repository is intended to be original and released under the repository's MIT license. Contributors must have the right to submit their work, and the MIT copyright and permission notice must be preserved in distributions.

MIT allows commercial use and resale, but also allows third parties to use and redistribute the same licensed code. It does not provide exclusivity or guarantee that the project is free of all legal risk.

To reduce avoidable risk:
1. Keep clean-room development records; do not paste source from competing trading bots into this project.
2. Review direct and transitive dependency licenses and archive an SBOM for each release.
3. Keep model runtimes and weights separate; review each model's license and redistribution restrictions.
4. Do not redistribute broker logos, market data, SDKs or examples unless their terms permit it.
5. Check the SCALPER name and logo for trademark conflicts before commercial launch.
6. Maintain contributor provenance and signed agreements if the project may be dual-licensed or sold exclusively later.

This is not legal advice and cannot guarantee no copyright claim. Consult qualified counsel before commercial distribution in relevant jurisdictions.
